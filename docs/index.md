# ToneSphere

**Route any audio anywhere: simple for everyone, deep enough for studios.**
Published by Neural Nexus Studios, Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

Plug a guitar into an audio interface, run it through the plugins you already own, and hear it
in your headphones, without a chain of virtual cables, a donation timer, or a driver panel from
2006. That is the whole idea.

## What it does

- Sorts everything that makes or takes sound into six bins (physical, virtual and application
  inputs and outputs) and connects them by dragging one onto another, through mixes with their
  own volume, effects and channel matrix.
- Hears your instrument in one click, through your audio interface's ASIO driver or Windows'
  own audio.
- Gives several DAWs and Guitar Rig one shared audio interface through the ToneSphere ASIO driver.
- Runs built-in effects (noise suppression, gate, EQ, compressor, limiter, auto-duck, delay) and
  your own VST3 effects and instruments, spread over several processor cores when they're heavy.
- Captures any app's sound and feeds any mix into an app's microphone.
- Records, streams over the network (VBAN), and takes orders from hotkeys, OSC, MIDI and scripts.
- In English and Hindi.

It runs entirely on your computer: no accounts, no telemetry, nothing collected.
