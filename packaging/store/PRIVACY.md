# ToneSphere privacy policy

_Effective: 2026-10-10_

ToneSphere does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data.

- **Audio.** ToneSphere processes audio only on your PC, to route it between the devices and
  apps you connect.
- **Network.** ToneSphere sends audio over the network only when you set up a VBAN stream
  yourself, and only to the address you enter.
- **Settings.** Your settings, scenes and recordings are stored in your user profile on
  your PC. They never leave it unless you copy them.
- **Analytics.** ToneSphere contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.
- **Local connection.** The app and its audio engine talk over a local connection
  (127.0.0.1) that is protected with a random token. They accept no connections from
  other computers.

Questions: open an issue in the ToneSphere source repository.
